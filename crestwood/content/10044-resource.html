Veterans & Military Student Resources

Crestwood University is proud to serve student veterans, active-duty service members, and military-affiliated students. Resources include GI Bill benefit processing, Yellow Ribbon Program participation, the Veterans Resource Center on campus, priority registration, and academic advising tailored to veterans' unique educational pathways. The Veterans Student Organization provides peer support and community connection.
