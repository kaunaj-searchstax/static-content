Student Union & Activities Center

The Student Union is the social and organizational hub of campus life at Crestwood University. It houses the Office of Student Activities, more than 200 registered student organizations, dining venues, the campus bookstore, meeting rooms, a game lounge, and the office of the Student Government Association. Events including concerts, cultural celebrations, and guest lectures are hosted year-round.
