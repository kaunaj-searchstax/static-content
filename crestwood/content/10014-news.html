Crestwood Ranked #1 for Undergraduate Research Opportunities

June 2026By Crestwood University Communications

For the third consecutive year, Crestwood University has been recognized as a top institution for undergraduate research opportunities by the Princeton Review. Over 60% of CU undergraduates engage in faculty-mentored research before graduation, with many co-authoring peer-reviewed publications. President Dr. Diana Marsh praised the university's commitment to integrating research into the undergraduate experience at all levels.
