Perkins Library & Learning Commons

The Perkins Library is Crestwood University's central research library, housing over 2.5 million volumes, extensive digital databases, rare manuscript collections, and collaborative study spaces. The adjacent Learning Commons offers writing assistance, tutoring, research consultations with subject librarians, and 24-hour study areas during finals periods.
