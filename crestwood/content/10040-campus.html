Office of Financial Aid — Main Campus

The Office of Financial Aid assists students and families in navigating all aspects of financial aid, including FAFSA completion, award letter review, appeals, verification, and scholarship applications. Walk-in assistance is available during business hours, and virtual appointments can be scheduled online. Dedicated counselors are assigned by the first letter of a student's last name for continuity of service.
