Main Campus — Crestwood, PA

Crestwood University's 340-acre main campus is located in Crestwood, Pennsylvania, featuring historic academic buildings, modern research facilities, student housing, a recreation center, and the nationally recognized Perkins Library. The campus is home to all undergraduate colleges and most graduate programs.
