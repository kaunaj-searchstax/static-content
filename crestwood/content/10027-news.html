5 things to know before choosing your major

May 2026By Crestwood Academic Success Center

Choosing a major is one of the most significant decisions you'll make at Crestwood, but it doesn't have to be stressful. In this article, advisors from the Academic Success Center share the five most important considerations — from career alignment and course load to double major and minor possibilities — and remind students that more than 30% of CU students switch majors at least once without losing academic standing.
