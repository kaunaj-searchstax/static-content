Undergraduate Admissions Guide

This guide walks prospective undergraduate students through every step of the Crestwood University application process, from selecting the right program to submitting your application materials. Learn about application deadlines (Early Decision: Nov 1; Regular Decision: Jan 15), required documents including transcripts and letters of recommendation, the role of standardized test scores in our holistic review, and how to schedule a campus visit. Information for international applicants and transfer students is included.
