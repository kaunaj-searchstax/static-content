Understanding your financial aid package

April 2026By Crestwood Office of Financial Aid

When your financial aid award arrives, it can be confusing to compare grants, scholarships, work-study, and loans. This guide from the Crestwood Financial Aid office explains what each type of aid means, how to accept or decline components of your package, what happens if your family's financial circumstances change, and how to appeal your award if you feel it doesn't reflect your family's true need.
