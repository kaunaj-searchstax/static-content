Crestwood Business School Launches New Entrepreneurship Hub

May 2026By Crestwood Business School Communications

The Crestwood Business School has opened the Langley Innovation Hub, a dedicated space for student entrepreneurs to develop and launch new ventures. The hub offers co-working space, access to seed funding through the Crestwood Venture Fund, mentorship from alumni founders, and connections to regional incubators and accelerators. The inaugural cohort of 20 student teams is currently in residence.
