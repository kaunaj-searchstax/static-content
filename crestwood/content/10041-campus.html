Office of Admissions — Main Campus

The Crestwood University Office of Admissions is the starting point for prospective students and families. Walk-in information sessions and campus tours are offered daily. Admissions counselors are available to answer questions about application requirements, program selection, and financial aid. The office coordinates undergraduate, graduate, transfer, and international admissions.
