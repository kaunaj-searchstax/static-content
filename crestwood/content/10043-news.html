Crestwood Eagles: 2025–26 athletics season highlights

June 2026By Crestwood Athletics Communications

The Crestwood Eagles had a historic 2025–26 athletics year, with the men's basketball team reaching the NCAA Tournament Sweet Sixteen, the women's soccer team claiming a Colonial Athletic Conference championship, and the swimming & diving program producing two All-Americans. Student attendance at home events hit a record high, with the men's basketball season selling out Harrington Arena four times.
