Study Abroad & Global Learning Programs

Crestwood University's Office of Global Education administers over 80 study abroad programs in more than 40 countries. Students can study abroad for a semester, academic year, or during summer and winter sessions. Program types include Crestwood-led faculty programs, exchange programs at partner universities, and approved independent programs. All CU financial aid applies to approved semester and year-long programs. Language immersion, research, and service-learning tracks are available.
