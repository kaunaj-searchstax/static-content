New Student Orientation & Welcome Week

New Student Orientation is your official introduction to Crestwood. First-year and transfer students attend a two-day summer orientation session (June–August) to register for fall classes, meet their academic advisor, and get their CU ID. Welcome Week runs the week before fall classes begin, with move-in, convocation, community events, and the student organization fair. Orientation registration opens in May after you submit your enrollment deposit; family sessions run alongside student programming.
