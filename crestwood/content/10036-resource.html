FAFSA & CSS Profile: Step-by-Step Application Guide

Applying for financial aid at Crestwood University requires completing either the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) or the CSS Profile, depending on the type of aid you are seeking. This step-by-step guide walks families through gathering the required financial documents, creating a StudentAid.gov account, understanding the difference between dependent and independent student status, and submitting your application on time to meet Crestwood's priority deadline of February 1. Guidance for families with unusual financial circumstances and for divorced or separated parents is included.
