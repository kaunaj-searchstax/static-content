IT Help Desk: Wi-Fi, Email, Passwords & Printing

The Crestwood IT Help Desk supports students with campus wifi (CU-Secure network) setup on laptops and phones, student email account access, password resets through the self-service portal, software downloads including free Microsoft 365, and campus printing from any device with CU Print. Walk-in support is available in the Perkins Library Learning Commons, and 24/7 phone and chat support covers urgent issues like a locked account or forgotten password.
