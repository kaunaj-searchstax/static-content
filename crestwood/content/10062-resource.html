Recreation & Wellness Center Hours & Memberships

Current operating hours for the Crestwood Recreation & Wellness Center. During the regular semester, the rec center and gym are open Monday–Friday 6 AM–11 PM and weekends 8 AM–9 PM, with the 25-meter pool, climbing wall, and group fitness studios running on posted sub-schedules. Access is free with your CU ID for enrolled students; faculty, staff, and alumni memberships are available. This guide covers holiday and break hours, fitness class registration, intramural sports sign-ups, locker rentals, and guest pass policies.
