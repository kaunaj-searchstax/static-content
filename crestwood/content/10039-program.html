BA in English Literature

Crestwood's English Literature program invites students to explore the full range of literary traditions — from medieval texts to contemporary global fiction — while developing exceptional skills in critical reading, analytical writing, and creative expression. Students may pursue a Creative Writing concentration, which includes workshops with published faculty authors. The program prepares graduates for careers in publishing, education, law, communications, and the arts.
