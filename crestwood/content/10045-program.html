Certificate in Data Analytics

The Certificate in Data Analytics is a non-degree credential designed for working professionals who want to build skills in data analysis, visualization, and basic machine learning without committing to a full degree program. The program consists of five courses and can be completed in as little as 12 months. Courses are offered online and at the Philadelphia Center on evenings and weekends.
