Applying to graduate school: a step-by-step guide

March 2026By Crestwood Pre-Graduate Advising

Thinking about graduate school? This comprehensive guide from Crestwood's pre-graduate advising team walks seniors and alumni through the entire process: selecting programs, requesting letters of recommendation, crafting a compelling statement of purpose, preparing for standardized tests, understanding funding and assistantships, and navigating offer decisions and deferral options. Timelines for fall admission typically start 12–18 months before enrollment.
